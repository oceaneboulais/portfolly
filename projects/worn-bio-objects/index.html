Project Overview

worn bio-objects is an ongoing exploration of wearable artifacts that blend organic and synthetic materials, inspired by marine and terrestrial forms. Each piece is designed to provoke questions about the boundaries between the body, technology, and the living world.

Concept & Process

The project draws from biomimicry, speculative design, and hands-on experimentation. Materials are sourced from both natural and recycled origins, and construction methods range from traditional handcraft to digital fabrication.

  • Material studies: algae, bioplastics, found objects
  • Wearability experiments: comfort, durability, interaction
  • Documentation: photography, field notes, user feedback

Reflections

"To wear a bio-object is to invite the world to touch you back."

This project is as much about process and play as it is about outcome. Each artifact is a conversation starter—about sustainability, identity, and the future of wearable technology.
