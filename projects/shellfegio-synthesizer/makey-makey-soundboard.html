Makey Makey browser soundboard

This page is built for physical key-style input in a browser, with 11 assignable sound slots, held-key looping, and a one-tap audio unlock flow that is safer on iPhone audio playback rules.

Best use: tap Enable audio once after the page opens, then press your Makey Makey keys. Files are assigned in order to A S D F G H J K L ; ' by default.

Each key loops while held. Releasing the key fades the sound out quickly to avoid clicks.

Setup notes

On iPhone, browser pages can use regular keyboard-style events and Web Audio after a real user gesture, but Web MIDI support itself is not available in iOS Safari-class browsers. If your Makey Makey appears to the phone as a keyboard, this page is the reliable path. If it appears only as a MIDI device, a standard browser page on iPhone will not be dependable.
