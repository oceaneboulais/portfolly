This dataset contains the sounds of three species of Balistidae : Balistapus undulatus, Rhinecanthus aculeatus and Rhinecanthus rectangulus.
All the information are in the article "Sound production mechanism in triggerfish (Balistidae): a synapomorphy" published in Journal of Experimental Biology in 2018 by
Xavier Raick, David Lecchini, Loïc Kéver, Orphal Colleye, Frédéric Bertucci and Eric Parmentier

Doi: 10.1242/jeb.168948
Link: https://journals.biologists.com/jeb/article/221/1/jeb168948/19562/Sound-production-mechanism-in-triggerfish

All the specimens are from Moorea Island (French Polynesia)
B. undulatus [standard length (SL) 10.59–15.35 cm]
R. aculeatus (SL: 12.7–17.4 cm) 
R. rectangulus (SL: 8.1–15.3 cm) 

Specimens were recorded hand-held in a glass aquarium with a hydrophone HTI-96-MIN (sensitivity: −163.9dB V μPa−1; High Tech Inc., Long Beach, MS, USA) connected to a TASCAM DR-07 recorder (TEAC, Wiesbaden, Germany).
The sounds were digitized at 44.1 kHz (16-bit resolution).
