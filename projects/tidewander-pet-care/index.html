These rates sit comfortably within typical market ranges for pet sitters — drop-in visits nationally tend to run roughly $20–$45/hour and overnight in-home care roughly $45–$100/night, with coastal California markets like San Diego trending toward the higher end of those bands. experience

Hands-on, not just well-meaning

Before this was a service, it was just life with fosters. That experience is where most of what I know about reading a dog's body language, patience, and routine actually comes from.

  • Fostered 5 dogs — hands-on day-to-day care, from intake to adoption.
  • 2 reactive-dog fosters taught me how to read triggers, manage distance and thresholds, use calm/structured handling, and avoid escalating a stressful situation — skills I bring to every reactive or anxious dog in my care.
  • Special needs dogs — comfortable with mobility aids, medication schedules, and adapted routines.
  • Senior dogs — gentle pacing, joint-friendly walks, and attentiveness to the small changes that matter with older pets.
