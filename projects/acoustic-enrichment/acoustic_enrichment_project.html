Supplemental Materials: Interactive Fish Larvae Observations

In support of manuscript under review, Scientific Reports (Nature): "Autonomous cameras observe enhanced reef fish larvae response to acoustic enrichment in natural field conditions over multiple lunar cycles"

For continuous visual monitoring, an open-source camera system was employed based on the ESP32-S chip and an OV2640 camera (2MP 1600 x 1200 resolution) with a 15 frames per second image transfer rate and 65-degree field of view (FoV). The artificial reef structures below were designed and 3D printed or casted by our co-authors at the Hawai'i Institute of Marine Biology's Geometric Ecology Lab. The science behind the structural designs can be explored more here.
