📖 Vocabulary Dive

Knowing Your Rocks: Key Terms

Sources: Sapkota et al. 2025 (arXiv:2505.10468); Händler 2023 (SCITEPRESS); Guo et al. 2024 (IEEE Access); MIT Sloan, 2026

LLM
Large Language Model

A neural network trained on massive text data that can read, write, reason, and generate. Think of it as the otter's brain — pattern recognition at scale. Examples: GPT-4, Claude, Gemini.

AI AGENT
Modular Task Executor

A software system that uses an LLM to complete a specific, bounded task — searching the web, writing code, sending an email. Like one otter with one rock cracking one type of shell.

AGENTIC AI
Orchestrated Autonomy

"Multi-agent collaboration, dynamic task decomposition, persistent memory, and coordinated autonomy" (Sapkota et al., 2025). The whole otter pod working together on a complex goal — planning, routing, adapting.

MAS
Multi-Agent System

Multiple AI agents coordinating together, each handling a sub-task. One finds papers, one writes code, one checks logic. Händler (2023) identifies this as the key leap beyond single-agent capability limits.

Autonomy Spectrum (Sapkota et al., 2025)
Generative AI
Responds when asked
→
AI Agent
Executes one task
→
Agentic AI
Plans + coordinates goals
🪨 Prompt Engineering

Choosing the Right Rock

The CORE Framework for Scientific Prompting

C
Context
Give background on your research domain, dataset size, methodology. The more the AI knows, the sharper the rock.
O
Output format
Specify: table, bullet list, Python function, LaTeX equation, 300-word draft. Vague prompts get vague answers.
R
Role
"You are a structural biologist reviewing a methods section." Role-assignment shifts the AI into specialist mode.
E
Exclusions
State what NOT to do: "Don't cite papers after 2022. Don't simplify the math. Don't repeat the question."
🦦 Lazy prompt: "Explain my results." → CORE prompt: "You are a computational neuroscientist. Interpret these regression coefficients [table] in the context of synaptic plasticity models. Output: 2 paragraphs, technical language, no introduction filler."
🧩 Advanced Patterns

Chaining Your Rocks

⛓️
Prompt Chaining: Break complex tasks into sequential steps. Step 1: "Identify all assumptions in this methods section." → Step 2: "For each assumption, suggest a robustness test."
🗺️
Plan-First Execution: Ask: "Before answering, outline your plan." Catches wrong approaches before wasted output. Critical for multi-file code tasks.
🔁
Upside-Down Critique: After generating a hypothesis, immediately ask: "What are the three strongest arguments against this?" Forces steel-manned opposition.
📎
Attachment Hygiene: Name files semantically: methods_chapter_v3.md not doc1.pdf. Use structured Markdown for complex inputs—agents perform better.

Part III: The Balance of the Reef

Ethics, integrity & the scientist's responsibility

⚖️ Ethics & Integrity

The Urchin Problem: When Otters Overeat

✅ Healthy Foraging (AI as Tool)

Literature search & summarization with citation verification
Debugging code you wrote and understand
Polishing prose, grammar, clarity — with disclosure
Brainstorming hypotheses that you then design experiments to test

🚫 Urchin Barren (AI as Replacement)

Fabricating, altering, or interpreting data with AI
Submitting AI-written sections without disclosure
Citing unverified AI-generated references
Letting AI do the thinking you need to develop yourself
Oxford/Cambridge Framework (2024, Nature Machine Intelligence): Three criteria — (1) Human vetting of accuracy, (2) Substantial human contribution, (3) Transparent disclosure. All AI use must be acknowledged in Methods or Acknowledgements.

🧬 What Only You Can Do

The Otter's Irreplaceable Role

What AI cannot generate
  • 🔬 Novel empirical data from real-world experiments
  • 🧠 Domain intuition built through years of messy lab work
  • 🌊 Recognition of when "surprising" results are real vs. artifact
  • 🗣️ Ethical accountability for published conclusions
  • 💡 Questions that reframe an entire field
The Keystone Parallel

Sea otters keep urchin populations in check. Remove them and you get urchin barrens — no kelp, no ecosystem.

Without human researchers maintaining rigor, AI produces an "urchin barren" of polished-but-shallow papers.

You are the keystone species of your research ecosystem.
